smart_context_menu

A powerful, fully customizable iOS-style context menu for Flutter: smart positioning, fluid animations, drag-to-select, rich submenus and your own content above the widget.

Add it to your app

flutter pub add smart_context_menu

Source on GitHub

Try it

Everywhere

  • Long press any file, message or card to open its menu.
  • Slide across the items and release on one.
  • Swipe right in a submenu to go back, as in iOS.
  • Tap the preview to open the file or the photo.

Files

  • The ⋯ button in a row opens the same menu.
  • Tap Download: the progress shows in the open menu.

Chat

  • Tap a reaction above a message.
  • Open Reactions of the lake photo: people load as you scroll.
  • Double tap next to a message to open its menu.
  • Photos fly to the photo page with their own Hero.

Appearance shows the menu in other styles. The gear sets the theme, haptics and slow animations.
